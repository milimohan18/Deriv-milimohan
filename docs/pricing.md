# Pricing and Plans

Nimbus offers three plans: Starter, Growth, and Enterprise.

## Starter

The Starter plan is free and includes 10,000 API requests per month, 1 workspace, and community support. It is intended for evaluation and hobby projects.

## Growth

The Growth plan costs $49 per month and includes 1,000,000 API requests per month, up to 5 workspaces, and email support. Additional requests are billed at $0.50 per 10,000 requests.

## Enterprise

Enterprise pricing is custom and includes a 99.95% uptime SLA, SSO, and a dedicated support engineer. Contact sales for a quote.

## Startup program

Startups that are less than 2 years old and have raised under $5M in funding can apply to the Nimbus Startup Program. Accepted startups receive the Growth plan free for 12 months. Applications are reviewed within 10 business days.

## Billing

All paid plans are billed monthly in USD. Annual billing is available with a 15% discount.
